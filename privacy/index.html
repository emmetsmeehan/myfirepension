Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

My Fire Pension is a free, unofficial planning tool for FDNY members. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is ever sold. This page covers the website and the My Fire Pension iPhone app.

What stays on your device

The dates and earnings you enter are saved in your browser (or in the app) on your own phone or computer, so they're there next time. "Clear my earnings" at the bottom of the personal numbers page erases the earnings; your dates stay.

W-2s, screenshots and paychecks you upload are read right on your device. The files themselves are never uploaded.

How the site is used

The site uses Microsoft Clarity to see how people use it, so I can fix what's confusing. Clarity records visits, taps, scrolling and page replays, along with your device and browser type and your rough location (country or region, from your internet address). It uses cookies to do this.

What you type into the boxes is hidden from the replays. When the page shows you a result, it sends Clarity a summary of the numbers behind it, such as the earnings for each year (including LODI and bonus amounts from W-2s), your final average salary, years of service, rank and the monthly pension shown. It never sends your name, date of birth or appointment date, and nothing from a W-2 besides those pay amounts.

This isn't tied to your name and isn't used for ads. Microsoft handles it under its privacy statement.

When you send a question

The question box sends your message and email address to me through a form service called Web3Forms. I use your email only to answer you.

Other services the pages load

  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare.
  • The font comes from Google Fonts.
  • The tools that read uploads come from jsDelivr, only when you upload something.

Like any website, these see your internet address when your device asks them for a file.

Questions or deletion

Email me at emmetsmeehan@gmail.com with any questions, or to have a message you sent deleted. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
