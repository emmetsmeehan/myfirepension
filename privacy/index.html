Privacy

For the myfirepension.com website and the Fire Pension iPhone app. Last updated Nov. 6, 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is sold. Your numbers are saved on your own phone or computer. A usage tool, Microsoft Clarity, shows me how people use the site and app so I can make them better.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (your dates, your earnings, the W-2s, screenshots and paychecks you add) is read and saved on your own phone or computer. Files you add are read right there and never uploaded. "Clear my numbers" at the bottom of the personal numbers page erases what's saved. Deleting the app does too.

What Microsoft Clarity sees

The site and the app use Microsoft Clarity to show me how they're used. Clarity collects:

  • Which pages and screens you open, where you tap and how far you scroll, including recordings of the screen while you use it
  • Your device, browser and approximate location (from your internet address)
  • Summary numbers from the calculators, like the salary, years of service, yearly earnings and monthly pension shown, so I can check the calculators are working

Your date of birth, appointment date and equated date are never sent, and neither is anything else from your W-2s. On the website, Clarity uses cookies to tell one visit from the next. Microsoft handles this data under its privacy statement.

When you send me a message

If you use the question box, your message and email address are sent to me through Web3Forms, a form service, so I can answer you. I don't use your email for anything else.

Other services the website loads

The website loads its font from Google Fonts, and the tools that read pictures and PDFs from jsDelivr, a public code library. They see your internet address like any website you visit, but never your numbers or files.
