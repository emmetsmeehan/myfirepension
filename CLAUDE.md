# My Fire Pension: FDNY Tier 2 and Tier 3 pension calculators

A public, single-page guide and pension calculator for FDNY uniformed members in Tier 3 (appointed on or after July 1, 2009). The owner is the GitHub user `emmetsmeehan`. He built it to share with fellow members, who will use it when thinking about real retirement decisions, so the numbers and the wording have to be right.

This repository is the source of truth. The calculator used to live on a claude.ai page; that copy is retired.

## Rules file and test cases (Nov. 7, 2026, owner's request)

- **`rules.js`** at the root holds every rule number the math uses, for both tiers: VSF, longevity bonus, COLA, rank top pay, 2026 federal tax brackets, Tier 3 pension %, 3/4s %, 110% cap, full escalation years (23) and the 36-month ramp, 3% escalation cap, Social Security offset, mandatory age; Tier 2 pension %, 1/60th, 120% cap, ITHP rates, mandatory age. Each calculator page loads it with `<script src="/rules.js">` before its main script (`RS`, `R3`/`R2` in the script). Change a rule number there and nowhere else. The iPhone app downloads `https://myfirepension.com/rules.js` at launch and parses everything after the last `window.RULES =` (minus the final `;`) as JSON, keeping its built-in copy as a fallback, so that part must stay strict JSON (the tests check it). Wording on the pages that quotes a number ("$12,000", "23 years", "3%") is not wired to the file: when a number changes, fix the wording too.
- **Test cases:** `node tests/run.mjs` (run `npm install` in `tests/` once). It loads each tier page in jsdom with a case's dates and earnings saved, runs the page's own `scenario()`, and checks monthly pension, FAS and escalation fraction. `tests/cases.json` are made-up members figured by hand from the rules above (independently of the page code). **Run the tests before every push; if one fails, fix it, and if you can't, don't push that change and tell the owner why** (owner's standing instruction).
- **Real members' Fund estimates** (from the Fund's member portal, collected by the owner) go in `tests/private-cases.json`, same format, which is gitignored and must never be committed: Cloudflare Pages serves this whole repo publicly (this file included), and pay plus dates identifies a member. ## How to work on it

- Make small, targeted edits. Do not rewrite the page to make a change. (The owner asked for one full redesign in Oct. 2026; that is done.)
- Most users are on phones and are not tech-savvy. Keep tap targets large, wording plain, and the answer close to the inputs. After a layout change, check about 390px and 320px wide, light and dark. The tight spots are the service slider's stop buttons (22 and 22½ sit side by side on the lower row), the earnings table and the comparison table.
- Every color is a CSS token defined for light and dark themes at the top of the stylesheet. Use the tokens; do not hard-code colors.
- After changing the script, confirm it parses, and test the affected formula at its boundaries (for example 24 years 11 months, 25, 29, 30 and 35 years for the longevity bonus).
- Check pay figures and legal claims against primary sources before putting them on the page. If something could not be verified, tell the owner plainly.
- When a rule changes, update every place that describes it: the hints in both calculators, the Pension basics answers, the fine print at the bottom of Pension basics, and the bill cards.
- The owner wants direct answers. When a request conflicts with a rule below, or the right reading is unclear, ask him before changing the math.

## Calculation rules

These are the rules the page implements. They are the owner's decisions; do not change them without asking.

- **Pension:** 50% of final average salary (FAS) at 20 or more years of service, under S4727 (Chapter 692 of the Laws of 2025, signed Dec. 19, 2025). Tier 3 is capped at 50%.
- **FAS:** the best 3 consecutive calendar years. (The Fund's Tier 3 Enhanced SPD, Dec. 2019 revision in the owner's Drive, says 3 years only for members who joined July 1, 2009 to March 31, 2012, and 5 years, each capped at 110% of the prior 4 years' average, for "modified Tier 3" members from April 1, 2012. The owner confirmed 3 years (Oct. 2026), though the April 2024 plan description says 5. The personal calculator uses full calendar years before the retirement year. **Exact best 3 with paychecks (Nov. 2, 2026, owner's request; Tier 3 only):** the SPD (Dec. 2019, p. 11) defines FAS over "any five consecutive years, based on the month and day of retirement": each year runs from the retirement month and day, anywhere in the career. Without paychecks the page keeps whole calendar years and says so ("(calendar years)" after the best 3 in the results; the folded "Find your exact best 3" button at the end of step 2 says the real ones can start on any date). The owner wants it acknowledged for everyone and the deep dive optional.
