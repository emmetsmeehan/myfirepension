Your other accounts.

Your pension isn’t the only money you retire with. Here’s what else you may have.

Voya NYC Deferred Compensation Plan

The City’s savings plan. Voya runs the website and app. In every account here, you choose your own investments from the plan’s menu of funds.

Pre-taxLowers your taxes now. You pay tax when you take it out.
or
RothNo tax break now. Comes out tax-free later, if the account is 5+ years old and you’re 59½.

Each account below comes in both. You can split your money between them and change it anytime.

457

From your paycheckPre-tax or Roth

No penalty at any age once you leave the job.Retire at 42 and you can use it the next day, with no 10% early-withdrawal penalty. It’s the only account here that can do that, which makes it the most flexible one for retiring early. You still pay income tax on pre-tax money.Money you roll in from a 401(k) or IRA keeps that account’s penalty rules.

You pick a percentage of each paycheck and the funds it goes into.

Up to $24,500 a year (2026)

Can I put in more?

Yes, at 50 and up:

Under 50$24,500
50 to 59, or 64 and up$32,500
60 to 63$35,750

That’s separate from the 401(k). You can max out both.

Close to retirement? In the 3 years before your “normal retirement age,” the 457 may let you put in up to double the limit to make up for past years. The plan calls it Deferral Acceleration for Retirement. Call the plan before using it.

Made over $150,000 last year? Any extra catch-up money at 50 and up has to go in as Roth.

2026 limits. The IRS raises them most years.

401(k)

From your paycheckPre-tax or Roth

Works like the 457, with its own separate limit. Use one, or both.

Penalty-free at 50, or after 25 years on the job.Retired firefighters can take money out without the 10% penalty at whichever comes first. Before that, the penalty can apply.

Up to $24,500 a year (2026)

Can I put in more?

Yes, at 50 and up:

Under 50$24,500
50 to 59, or 64 and up$32,500
60 to 63$35,750

That’s on top of the 457. With both, someone under 50 can put in $49,000 a year.

Made over $150,000 last year? Any extra catch-up money at 50 and up has to go in as Roth.

2026 limits. The IRS raises them most years.

NYCE IRA

Paycheck or by checkTraditional or Roth

You may already have a traditional or Roth IRA on the side, at a bank or a brokerage. This is the same kind of account, with the same limit. If you don’t have one, you can open one here through the City, for free, with the same low-cost funds as the 457 and 401(k). Spouses can open one too.

No need to move your 457 here when you retire.Your 457 stays yours after you retire. Moving it into an IRA before 59½ loses its no-penalty rule.

Up to $7,500 a year (2026)

Can I put in more?

Yes, at 50 and up:

Under 50$7,500
50 and up$8,600

That limit is for all your IRAs together, so it’s shared with any IRA you have somewhere else. It doesn’t count against the 457 or 401(k).

You can put money in through payroll (since July 2025) or by check. A Roth IRA has income limits; a high household income can rule it out.

2026 limits.

Empower Union annuity

Separate from Voya, the pension and deferred comp. Your union’s annuity fund holds it, and Empower runs the website.

Union annuity

The City puts money in

An investment account the City pays into for you, through the UFA or UFOA. You don’t put in anything.

Check what it’s invested in.The UFA annuity starts out in a very conservative fund, and many members never change it. You can log in to Empower and pick more aggressive funds.

What the City puts in each year (spread out over 13 payments)

  • When you’re promoted, it moves from the UFA’s fund to the UFOA’s.

Already part of your pension: the VSF and banked variable.

$12,000 a year after 20 years, plus a lump sum for each year you work past 20. These aren’t separate accounts, and the pension calculators already include them.

A few more things

Can I borrow from it?

Yes, from the pre-tax money in the 457 or 401(k), not the Roth. You pay it back through payroll, with interest that goes back into your own account.

Money you borrow isn’t invested while it’s out, and leaving the job with a loan unpaid can make it taxable.

Can I use it to buy back time for my pension?

Yes. Pre-tax 457 or 401(k) money can pay for a pension buyback, such as military time, moved straight to the Fire Pension Fund with no tax. Roth money can’t.

When do I have to start taking money out?

At 73 for pre-tax money. That’s when the IRS makes you take a minimum amount each year. Roth money in the plan never has to come out.

What does Voya cost?

$20 a quarter (waived your first year), charged once even if you have both plans, plus 0.04% a year on your balance. The funds’ own fees are low too: the S&P 500 index fund costs about 0.04% a year.

Who gets my money if I die?

Whoever you named as beneficiary. You name them separately for each Voya account and for the union annuity. Check yours after a marriage, divorce or new baby.

Facts from the NYC Deferred Compensation Plan’s summary guide (Feb. 2025), the IRS 2026 limits and the UFOA contract with the City (annuity fund article). This page explains how the accounts work. It isn’t advice on what to do with your money.
