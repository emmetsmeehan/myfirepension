Which tier are you in?

Tier 2 Appointed July 1, 1973 to June 30, 2009 Half pay at 20 years, plus a little more for each year after Tier 3 Appointed on or after July 1, 2009 Half pay at 20 years; grows with inflation from 23 years

Transferred in from another NY public pension system (like NYCERS as an EMT) before July 1, 2009? You may be Tier 2 even if you were appointed later. Not sure? Call the Fire Pension Fund: 929-436-0099.
