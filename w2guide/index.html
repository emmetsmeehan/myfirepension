Get your W-2s from ESS

Every W-2 you've had with the City is in NYCAPS Employee Self Service (ESS).

1. Log in to ESS. Scroll down and tap Electronic W2 Delivery.

2. The Tax Summary comes up very small. Pinch to zoom in, then tap a year.

You can screenshot this entire page to save time, but it won’t be completely accurate because it doesn’t include LODI. You’ll get a more accurate estimate by screenshotting your W-2s.

To get your W-2:

3. That year's Tax Details opens. It has every box from your W-2, including LODI.

The circled boxes are the two that matter for your pension: Medicare Wages (your earnings for the year) and Lodi (pay while out on line-of-duty injury, which counts too). Example numbers shown.

4. Take a screenshot of the whole page, zoomed out so every box fits. Do the same for each year you need. Then upload them in Your personal numbers: in step 2, tap Upload your W-2s.
